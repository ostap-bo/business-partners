A Top-25 Audit Firm With 15 Years on the Ukrainian Market

We support companies across a wide range of industries while upholding high professional standards, legal requirements, International Standards on Auditing, and our code of ethics. Business Partners ranks among Ukraine's top 25 audit firms and brings many years of experience in financial statement audits, including mergers and acquisitions, consolidation, and the transformation of reporting to international standards. The company is listed in the Register of Auditors and Audit Entities (Sections 3 and 4). Our clients receive not only quality audit work but a full range of consulting solutions to strengthen financial stability, improve management efficiency, and attract investment.

Mission and Values

Our mission is to meet the highest quality standards, continuously develop our professional expertise, and apply best practice. We combine hands-on experience with a deep understanding of industry specifics, the domestic market, and Ukrainian legislation.

Our History

Business Partners Audit and Consulting Firm is a team of professionals that has worked on the Ukrainian market since 2011 in audit, consulting, legal and tax services. We also specialize in implementing International Financial Reporting Standards and help businesses structure and optimize their operations.

Business Partners LLC is 100% owned by its participants (partners), who are Ukrainian citizens and employees of the firm. The firm is not a member of any audit network.

Business Partners LLC adheres to sound standards of corporate governance. Its highest governing body is the General Meeting of Participants, whose responsibilities include defining the firm's core areas of activity, developing strategy, appointing those responsible for the quality management system, and deciding on the distribution of net profit.

The firm is listed in the Register of Auditors and Audit Entities maintained by the Audit Public Oversight Body (APOB): in Section 3 of the register ("Audit entities entitled to conduct statutory audits of financial statements") and Section 4 ("...entities of public interest"). The firm has once again successfully passed an audit quality control review, confirmed by APOB Order No. 36-kya dated 22.06.2026.
