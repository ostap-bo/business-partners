    Our Process

    01

    Client and Engagement Assessment

    We analyze the nature of the client's business, assess independence, and identify potential risks and conflicts of interest.

    02

    Team Competency Assessment

    We confirm the team has enough qualified specialists with the right experience and resources.

    03

    Planning

    We use working paper templates developed in line with professional standards and regulatory requirements.

    04

    Engagement Execution

    We carry out the work in accordance with International Standards on Auditing and Ukrainian legislation.

    05

    Quality Review

    An independent partner not involved in the engagement performs a quality review before the report is signed.

    06

    Report Delivery

    We issue the report (and, if needed, an additional report to the audit committee) based on the results of our work.
