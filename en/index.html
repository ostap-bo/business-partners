About Us

Business Partners has worked on the Ukrainian market since 2011 in audit, consulting, legal and tax services. The firm specializes in financial statement audits, including mergers and acquisitions, consolidation, and the transformation of financial statements to international standards. We help businesses strengthen financial stability, improve management efficiency and create the right conditions to attract investment.

Business Partners ranks among Ukraine's top 25 audit firms and has more than 15 years of practice. The company is listed in the Register of Auditors and Audit Entities.
