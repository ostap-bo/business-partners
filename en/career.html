An Internship That Opens the Door to a Career in Auditing

The chance to ask practitioners questions and hear real stories about the profession is often the moment that helps students decide on their future direction. These conversations help not only to learn about the internship itself, but also to get a real feel for which professional path truly resonates.

Working at Business Partners

The firm considers its auditors and specialists to be its greatest asset. Technical skills and professional qualities are the foundation of our success. We're confident that the quality of our work rests on the experience, skills, dedication, and drive of our people, so we focus on careful hiring and staff development, fair compensation, and the smart allocation of resources to get the job done.

We've implemented high standards for hiring, candidate interviews, and qualification requirements. We give preference to candidates with references or performance records from previous employers who are motivated to learn and reach new levels of qualification. Recruiting and retaining talented auditors and specialists, and giving them broad opportunities to grow, is one of our key priorities.

We continuously update our technological and intellectual resources to keep pace with the digital world, and we rethink our workflows and methodologies to better support our audit teams.

The company continually invests financial resources and time in training and raising employees' professional level - through participation in in-person and online events and on-the-job mentoring.

Current Openings

Audit Assistant

Full-time

Responsibilities

  • Participating in audit engagements under the guidance of a senior auditor
  • Reviewing source documents and preparing audit working papers
  • Helping gather and analyze information for the audit report
  • Communicating with the client's accounting team within the scope of the engagement

Requirements

  • A university degree (or final-year studies) in Accounting and Taxation, Finance, or a related economics field
  • A basic understanding of accounting and financial statements
  • Confident Excel user
  • Attention to detail and a willingness to learn

Conditions

  • Official employment
  • Mentoring and training on real projects
  • Office in Lviv, hybrid format available

Corporate Legal Counsel

Full-time

Responsibilities

  • Legal review of incorporation documents, contracts, and minutes
  • Drafting charters, contracts, powers of attorney and other documents
  • Supporting the registration/re-registration of legal entities
  • Advising clients on corporate law matters

Requirements

  • A university degree in Law
  • Understanding of corporate law and business registration procedures
  • Strong business writing skills and the ability to draft legal documents
  • Prior experience is an advantage but not a requirement

Conditions

  • Official employment, competitive pay based on interview results
  • Support for professional development
  • Office in Lviv

Intern (Internship Program)

Internship, part-time/flexible

Responsibilities

  • Joining an audit team as an assistant
  • Working with source documents under a mentor's supervision
  • Getting hands-on with the firm's audit methodology and standards

Requirements

  • A student in years 3-6, or a graduate of a relevant field (exceptionally, from year 2)
  • Motivation to gain practical experience in the audit profession
  • Basic knowledge of Excel and accounting

Conditions

  • Official employment, an entry in your employment record, salary paid to your bank card
  • A flexible schedule starting from 4 hours a day - agreed during the interview
  • Hybrid format for students studying in Lviv
